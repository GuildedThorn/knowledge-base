# Ransomware Response and Recovery

## Purpose

Ransomware response is a coordinated containment, evidence, identity, restoration, legal, and communications problem.

## Key Ideas

- Immediate goals: stop spread, preserve evidence, protect backups, identify initial access, and understand what data left the environment.
- Credential resets before containment can warn an active actor and cause them to accelerate; sequence matters.
- Recovery is not just decrypt/restore: rebuild trust in identity, endpoints, network paths, and monitoring.

## Defensive Use

- Use pre-approved decision trees for isolation, shutdown, ransom communications, law enforcement, insurer, counsel, and customer/regulator notifications.
- After restoration, continue threat hunting for persistence and monitor for re-extortion or re-entry through the same access path.
