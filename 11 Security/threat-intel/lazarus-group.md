# Lazarus Group

## Purpose

Lazarus is a DPRK-attributed umbrella for espionage, destructive operations, and financially motivated cyber activity.

## Key Ideas

- Public reporting treats Lazarus as an umbrella with overlapping DPRK units, shared tooling, infrastructure, and personnel.
- Campaign themes include destructive wipers, cryptocurrency theft, defense/aerospace targeting, social engineering, and cross-platform malware.
- Attribution confidence varies by campaign; track clusters and tradecraft separately from geopolitical labels.

## Defensive Use

- Hunt for recruiter/job-lure delivery, signed or trojanized developer tools, macOS/Windows cross-platform payloads, and cryptocurrency workflow abuse.
- Use ATT&CK group mappings as a starting point, then validate against current vendor reports before operationalizing IOCs.
