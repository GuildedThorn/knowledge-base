# FIN7

## Purpose

FIN7 is a financially motivated intrusion cluster that moved from point-of-sale theft toward big-game ransomware operations.

## Key Ideas

- FIN7 has targeted retail, hospitality, finance, software, cloud, healthcare, and other sectors since at least 2013.
- Public reporting links the cluster to phishing, lookalike domains, POS malware history, Carbanak-adjacent tooling, and later ransomware ecosystems.
- Its history is useful for tracking the convergence of fraud crews, intrusion operators, and ransomware affiliate models.

## Defensive Use

- Monitor phishing infrastructure, signed or renamed remote-management tooling, credential theft, discovery scripts, and unusual payment/POS access.
- Treat FIN7-style intrusions as business-process compromise as much as endpoint compromise: payment flows and privileged SaaS matter.
