# Threat-Informed Defense and D3FEND

## Purpose

Threat-informed defense maps real adversary behavior to defensive controls, using ATT&CK for behavior and D3FEND for countermeasure vocabulary.

## Key Ideas

- ATT&CK describes observed adversary tactics and techniques; D3FEND models defensive techniques and their relationships to artifacts and attacks.
- D3FEND 1.0 provides a stable cybersecurity ontology for security architecture, control mapping, and coverage reasoning.
- The value is vocabulary plus mapping: teams can discuss what a control actually does instead of relying on vendor category names.

## Defensive Use

- Use ATT&CK to define what behavior must be detected or prevented, then use D3FEND to identify concrete defensive technique classes.
- Track coverage by technique and data source; avoid treating a control purchase as proof of behavior coverage.
