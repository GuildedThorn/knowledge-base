# Incident Response Lifecycle

## Purpose

NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 reframes incident response as CSF 2.0-aligned risk management across Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.

## Key Ideas

- Rev. 3 supersedes the older Rev. 2 incident-handling guide and aligns incident response with CSF 2.0.
- Preparation is broader than a response phase: Govern, Identify, and Protect activities support later Detect, Respond, and Recover work.
- Lessons learned should feed continuous improvement across all functions, not sit in a postmortem archive.

## Defensive Use

- Maintain playbooks per incident type, asset class, containment tradeoff, evidence source, and notification duty.
- Exercise response plans with tabletop and technical drills; untested playbooks are assumptions.
