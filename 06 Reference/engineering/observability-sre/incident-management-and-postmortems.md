# Incident Management and Postmortems

## Purpose

Incident management coordinates detection, roles, communication, mitigation, and learning without turning postmortems into blame sessions.

## Core Model

- Incident roles usually include incident commander, operations/technical leads, communications, and scribe.
- Timeline quality determines whether follow-up work addresses real causes or vague memories.
- Blameless postmortems focus on system conditions and decision context, not personal fault.

## Engineering Notes

- Declare incidents early when coordination cost is lower than confusion cost.
- Keep status updates factual: impact, mitigation, ETA if known, next update time.
- Track action items to closure; postmortems without ownership become theater.
