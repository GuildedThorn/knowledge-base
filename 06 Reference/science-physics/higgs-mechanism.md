# The Higgs Mechanism

## Purpose

Spontaneous electroweak symmetry breaking via the Higgs field gives mass to the W and Z bosons and other particles.

## Core Model

- A complex scalar doublet with a "Mexican hat" potential acquires a nonzero vacuum expectation value (about 246 GeV).
- This spontaneously breaks SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y down to the electromagnetic U(1)_EM.
- Three Goldstone modes are absorbed as the longitudinal polarizations of the W+, W-, and Z bosons, giving them mass.
- The photon stays massless because the residual U(1)_EM symmetry is unbroken.

## Consequences

- Fermion masses arise from Yukawa couplings to the same field, proportional to the vacuum expectation value.
- One physical excitation remains: the neutral scalar Higgs boson.
- A Higgs boson of mass about 125 GeV was observed at the LHC (ATLAS and CMS) in 2012.
- The 2013 Nobel Prize in Physics recognized Englert and Higgs for the theory.
