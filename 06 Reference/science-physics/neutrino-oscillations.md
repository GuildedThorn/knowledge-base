# Neutrino Oscillations

## Purpose

Neutrinos change flavor as they propagate, proving they have nonzero mass and mix via the PMNS matrix.

## How It Works

- Flavor eigenstates (electron, muon, tau) are quantum superpositions of mass eigenstates.
- The Pontecorvo-Maki-Nakagawa-Sakata (PMNS) matrix relates the flavor and mass bases via three mixing angles and a CP phase.
- Because mass eigenstates propagate with different phases, the flavor content oscillates with distance and energy.
- Oscillation probability depends on mass-squared splittings (delta m^2), not absolute masses.

## Experimental Evidence

- The solar neutrino deficit and atmospheric muon-neutrino disappearance were the first strong signals.
- Two independent splittings are measured: a smaller "solar" and a larger "atmospheric" delta m^2.
- Reactor and accelerator experiments pinned down the mixing angles, including nonzero theta_13.
- The 2015 Nobel Prize went to Kajita (Super-Kamiokande) and McDonald (SNO) for establishing oscillations and thus neutrino mass.
