# Quantum Chromodynamics and Asymptotic Freedom

## Purpose

QCD describes the strong force via colored quarks and gluons whose coupling weakens at short distance and confines at long distance.

## Core Model

- QCD is a non-Abelian gauge theory with symmetry group SU(3)_C, where the charge is called color.
- Quarks carry color; the eight gluons are the gauge bosons and themselves carry color charge.
- Gluon self-interaction, absent in electromagnetism, drives the theory's distinctive behavior.
- Physical states are color-neutral (color-singlet) hadrons such as baryons and mesons.

## Running Coupling and Confinement

- The strong coupling runs: it becomes small at high energy / short distance (asymptotic freedom).
- Asymptotic freedom lets perturbative QCD describe high-energy scattering and deep inelastic processes.
- At low energy / large distance the coupling grows, confining quarks and gluons inside hadrons.
- Gross, Politzer, and Wilczek received the 2004 Nobel Prize for discovering asymptotic freedom.
