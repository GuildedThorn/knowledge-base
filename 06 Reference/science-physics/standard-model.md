# The Standard Model of Particle Physics

## Purpose

The Standard Model is a gauge quantum field theory unifying the strong, weak, and electromagnetic interactions of known particles.

## Gauge Structure

- Built on the gauge symmetry group SU(3)_C x SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y.
- SU(3)_C is the color group of quantum chromodynamics; SU(2)_L x U(1)_Y is the electroweak sector.
- Electroweak symmetry breaks to U(1)_EM, leaving a massless photon and massive W and Z bosons.
- Interactions arise from local gauge invariance, which fixes the couplings of the force-carrier fields.

## Matter Content

- Fermions come in three generations of quarks (up/down type) and leptons (charged lepton plus neutrino).
- Left-handed fermions form SU(2) doublets; right-handed fermions are singlets, giving the theory its chiral structure.
- Force carriers are spin-1 gauge bosons: eight gluons, W+, W-, Z, and the photon.
- The scalar Higgs boson supplies masses via spontaneous symmetry breaking and Yukawa couplings.
